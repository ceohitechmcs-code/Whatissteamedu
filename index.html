WHAT IS STEAM?

Can five disciplines come together to build one real-world solution?

STEAM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics. It is an interdisciplinary approach to learning that connects knowledge with creativity, experimentation, design and real-world problem-solving.

🔬

SCIENCE

Understand how the world works through observation and experiments.

💻

TECHNOLOGY

Use and create digital tools, coding, AI and smart systems.

⚙️

ENGINEERING

Design, build, test and improve practical solutions.

🎨

ARTS

Bring imagination, creativity and human-centred design.

📐

MATHEMATICS

Measure, calculate, analyse and understand patterns.

In this interactive experience, you will explore a robotic arm, calculate its torque, program its movement, investigate its circuit, customise its gripper and experiment with joint angles. Each activity reveals how all five STEAM disciplines work together.
